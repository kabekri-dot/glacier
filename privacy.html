Privacy policy

Glacier collects nothing. It has no server of its own, no account, no analytics and no ads. Your notes are yours.

Last updated on 13 October 2026.

Where your notes are kept

Your notes and settings are saved in your browser, on your computer. They leave it only if you choose a place for them under Settings, Where your notes live:

  • Google Drive: Glacier saves your notes as Markdown files in a folder named Glacier in your own Google Drive.
  • A folder on this computer: Glacier saves your notes as files in the folder you pick. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • GitHub: Glacier saves your notes as files in the GitHub repository you name, with the access key you give it.

Glacier sends your notes only to the place you chose, directly from your browser. Nobody else, including the maker of Glacier, receives or can read them.

Google user data

If you choose Google Drive, Glacier asks Google for one permission: to see, create and change only the files Glacier itself makes in your Drive (drive.file). It cannot see any other files in your Drive, your email or your contacts.

Glacier uses this access only to save your notes there and read them back, so your notes are the same on your devices. The sign-in key from Google is kept in your browser only. Glacier does not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone, does not use it for ads, and no person reads it.

Glacier's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can stop it at any time: choose another place in Glacier's settings, or remove Glacier's access in your Google account. Your files stay in your Drive until you delete them.

The pages you visit

To open the right note for a website, Glacier can read the address and title of the tab you are on, if you allow it. It keeps them only inside the notes you write for that page. It never reads, saves or sends the content of the pages you visit. When you clip text from a page yourself, that text goes into your note.

Removing your data

Removing Glacier from your browser removes everything it saved there. Files in your Google Drive, folder or GitHub repository stay until you delete them.

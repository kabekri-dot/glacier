Glacier

A personal notebook in your browser's sidebar, for taking and connecting notes while you learn online.

What it does

  • A note is a page you write on, in Markdown, with links between notes.
  • Notes for a website open by themselves when you come back to it.
  • Collections keep notes on one topic together.
  • Your notes stay yours: in your browser, and if you like in your own Google Drive, a folder on your computer or your own GitHub repository, as plain Markdown files.

Privacy

Glacier has no server and collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.

Penguin break

The first hidden game, for Glacier paper. Click the empty line in the note, type /break and press Enter.

  • Space, ↑ or a click: jump over cracks and ice blocks. Hold it to jump higher and wider.
  • Every metre is a point, a fish 10, a rare golden fish 50
  • P pauses, Esc goes back to the note
  • Your top 5 shows after each round (in this sketch it's kept in this browser only)

Smooth movement, flat colours, nothing that flashes. It gets faster and the sky turns to evening and night the further you slide.

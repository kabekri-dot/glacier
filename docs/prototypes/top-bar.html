Quick actions, and details beside the note

⋯ drops down with the quick actions. Note details slides a panel over the paper with everything about the note: its collection, secret, page and the notes that link to it.

A piece of Settings
  • Click the title to see the headings. Hover it for the pen.
  • Click the pushpin on the paper.
  • Open ☰ Notes: type to search, open a collection, hover a note to rename or delete it.
  • In ☰ Notes, ⤢ expands the list over the whole sidebar.
  • In ⋯, try Delete, then Note details.
  • In Note details, change the collection and Secret.
  • At the end of the paper: New page, or × to hide it.
  • From two pages on, the pages and + New page sit under the bar.
